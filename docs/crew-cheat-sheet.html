The Crew Cheat Sheet

Everything you need to use the trip app — in one page.
1Get in
Open dr-babyyy.vercel.app → tap your name → set a 4-digit passcode (remember it). Add it to your home screen for the full PWA feel — once you're in, the app remembers you.
2The four tabs
🏖️
Home
Your profile, itinerary, holiday name & lockscreen
💬
Wall
Post photos, react with any emoji, reply
🎯
Games
Challenges + the daily scavenger hunt
🏆
Board
Daily + Holiday rankings, end-of-day ceremony
🎯 Games · Challenges
  • Pick a challenge → snap a photo → claim it
  • 2 vouches = verified, points lock in
  • Anyone can dispute if it's bogus
  • Easy 5–15pts · Medium 20–60 · Wild 70+
🎰 Games · Scavenger
  • 4 items revealed daily on a slot-machine spin
  • Find each one + upload a photo to claim
  • 1st finder gets a bonus 🥇
  • Day 1 is practice — doesn't count to Holiday total
🏆 Leaderboard · The daily ceremony
During the day
  • Daily board: today's points only
  • Holiday board: running trip total
  • Tap a row to drill into someone's day
End of day
  • Admin locks the day → 🎡 wheel spins for the loser's forfeit
  • Flow State Vote: crown the day's MVP — bonus points 👑
  • Next day begins · holiday names refresh
⚠️ Fouls · How not to be a snake
  • Anyone can log a foul on someone — points come off
  • The victim can dispute it · the logger can undo it
  • You can't vouch your own foul (we tried, we know)
  • Keep it fair, keep it funny — the wheel sees everything
✨ Pro moves
  • Set a Holiday Name — it shows up everywhere instead of your real name
  • Add a Catchphrase — shows under your name on the board + your hero
  • Generate a Lockscreen — phone wallpaper with your character + the trip dates
  • The welcome checklist on your home page ticks off as you try each feature
